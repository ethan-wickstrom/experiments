# Ledger

Every open item of the typeface (a gap, an idea, a deferral, a question, a request to the owner) and every closed one with its resolution. An entry's status changes in the same commit as the change that affects it; entries are closed, never deleted. G is a gap in what exists, I an idea not started, REQ a request to the owner.

## Open

### Gaps

- **G2 · Kerning and mark anchors do not vary across the design space.** They are computed at the Regular of the text size and applied at every weight and optical size, and in the static family of the small size (T17, T20). The compiler would store per-master values as deltas; `kern::values` already computes them per master (`rachis kern --own` shows them: To is −61 in the Bold, where the fonts have −77).
- **G3 · A few glyphs of some static fonts keep overlapping contours**, because the boolean union went wrong on them and the raster check caught it (T23): 52 glyph instances of 55,144 on 2026-11-15, six of them in Rachis Math (46 of 51,670 before that font; 42 before the signs of the proportional family were widened), listed by `rachis build`. Moving the contours apart by a hair, and uniting them one at a time, were tried and cured none.
- **G4 · Glyphs their authors called weak**, to be redrawn: `⎈ ⎋ ⌘ ⌨` in the Bold; `⅒`; `ₘ ᵐ` at 13 px in the Bold; subscripts with descenders reach −272, below the line box; `℘ ℜ 𝕎 𝕨 ℑ ℅`; `ϑ Ϗ ν`; `⇔ ⇕ ⟺` in the Bold at 13 to 16 px, `↝ ↜ ↭ ⇴ ⇼ ↠` in the Bold; `⊰ ⊱ ≼ ≽ ⋞ ⋟ ⟕ ⟖ ⟗ ⩴ ⋎ ⊜ ∜`; `æ` in the fixed pitch, `ð`, `Ǻ ǻ`, the cedilla's hook; the control pictures, `₧ ₨ ✂ ❅ ₾`; the Powerline branch sign at 13 px, the spinner frames, the shades at 13 to 14 px. `ϵ` and `∈` are close at 13 px.
- **G5 · Characters of the coverage survey that are not drawn**: 204 of the 873 of tier 2 (`research/`; tier 1's 1405 are all drawn): Cyrillic (124); arrows `⤊ ⤋ ⟰ ⟱ ⇮ ⇯ ⇶ ↫ ↬ ↯ ➦ ⟴`; the script capitals `ℊ ℋ ℐ ℒ ℛ ℬ ℰ ℱ ℳ ℴ 𝒫`, the Fraktur `ℌ ℨ ℭ`, `ℼ ⅀ ℶ ℷ ℸ`, the Ångström sign; the three-letter control pictures (20), `₯ ☈`; `⎉ ⟅ ⟆`; U+034D; `ϐ` and the archaic Greek numerals; the Roman numerals `Ⅰ` to `Ⅵ`, `↊ ↋`; the enclosed letters.
- **G6 · Box drawing at small sizes.** Where a line's edge falls part-way across a pixel, the overshoot of two neighbouring cells covers it twice and the join shows as a darker bead (T22); the shades alias into a plaid at 13 to 17 px. Hinting the lines to the pixel grid would cure the first.
- **G7 · Hinting.** The static fonts are hinted by ttfautohint and have been looked at only through FreeType; nothing has been looked at on Windows. The variable fonts are unhinted.
- **G8 · fontbakery's findings on the final fonts** (`check-universal`, version 1.1.0, on the two upright variable fonts, 2026-11-15: 181 PASS, 13 WARN, 6 FAIL, no ERROR). Four FAILs come from checking two families in one run (family names and axes differ between Rachis and Rachis Mono, as they should). The fonts' own: `usWinAscent` 1000 and `usWinDescent` 250 are less than the 1100 and 350 that box-drawing lines and bracket pieces reach, because those overshoot the line box so that neighbouring lines join (T13, T22); where a renderer clips at the win metrics it clips only the overshoot. Warnings to look at: the start points of one contour of `⇳` and of `⦾` differ between the Light and the Bold; `uni0312.case` is not reachable; `numberOfHMetrics` is 1983 in the fixed pitch because zero-advance marks are not first in the glyph order; no `avar` (the axes are linear); the carons of `ď ľ ť Ľ` want a look by eye. The italics and the static text fonts have not been run. On `ttf/RachisMath-Regular.ttf` the same profile gives 84 PASS, 5 WARN and no FAIL (2026-11-15): the carons again, contour counts that differ from the usual, signs wider than a figure (T27), a doubled segment in two forms for scripts (`u1D466.s1`, `uni03BC.s1`, the second among G3's), and the soft hyphen. The OpenType sanitiser accepts the font and its WOFF2.
- **G14 · Tests of the compiler cover the proportional face only through `build.rs`** (axes, advances, GPOS features, the kerning's behaviour). No test reads a kerning value back from a compiled font, or compiles the italics or a static font of Rachis.
- **G15 · Kerning of punctuation against letters is crude**: a colon before o is closed by 30 units because only the rows of its two dots count.
- **G16 · The combining overline, low line and long solidus attach only to letters and figures** (the `rule` anchor of the modules lower, upper, digits, greek, latin, letterlike). After a sign or an arrow they are still set over the following character in shaped text.
- **G9 · The proportional family's details.** Parentheses, brackets and braces are the fixed pitch's shapes, wide for a text face; `ď ľ ť` are loose; the Greek letters' widths are stated as cells (`Fit::letter`), not as sidebearings and ink, and the tail of `ρ` hangs into the sidebearing; the three-per-em and other spaces are not kerned against. Signs that still have the figure's cell after T27 and were not looked at: the keys and interface signs (`⌘ ⌥ ⏎ ⌫` and the rest of `technical.rs` but its delimiters), the pictographs of `shapes.rs`, `℮ ℅ ⅋ ℧ ℞ 𝕎 ℵ ℘` among the letterlike signs, `‴ ⁗ ⁄ ‿ ⁀ ‽`, `#` and `¶`. The combining arrows and dots over letters (U+20D6, U+20D7, U+20E1, U+20DB, U+20DC) are the fixed pitch's size, about two thirds of what mathematics fonts draw. The multiple integrals `∬ ∭ ∯ ∰` have their ink between the margins and their strokes are still packed as in the cell. Signs are not kerned against letters or each other.
- **G10 · Proofing coverage.** The Small masters and the Text Light and Bold were looked at as lines of text, not glyph by glyph; many glyphs drawn on 2026-11-15 were proofed in the Regular and the Bold of the fixed pitch only (each module's author listed what was not proofed).
- **G11 · The proof tool draws a zero-advance mark in the cell after its base**, so bare combining marks cannot be proofed in place.
- **G13 · The specimen page still copies some numbers by hand** (`specimen/specimen.html`): the lowest and highest reference distance of each pair in its chart (from `measurements.md`; they change only if the references do), the glyph counts and the time of a build in its terminal block, and the three counts in the note under its formulas (from `rachis math`). Rachis's own distances and the fonts' sizes are filled in by `rachis specimen` since 2026-11-15 (T29).
- **G17 · 71 targets of `measurements.md` are not met** in the three Regulars (2026-11-15; 89 before T28). None fails a check (T29). In the fixed-pitch Regular 11 of the 122 watched pairs are nearer than in every reference face that has the pair, and 24 are further apart than in every one. The 11, with the references' range: `ℝ R` 0.194 [0.264 to 0.337], `ℚ Q` 0.221 [from 0.232] and `ℂ C` 0.251 [from 0.261] (the Bold's cell limits the white of a doubled stroke, T28); `≤ ≦` 0.211 [0.312 to 0.394]; `1 7` 0.394 [from 0.425]; `∂ δ` 0.327 [from 0.342]; `⊂ ⊆` 0.397 [from 0.444]; and `v y`, `' "`, `^ ∧`, `vv w`, each under by less than 0.06 and far apart in any case. Inside the references' range and under the target, to be looked at as forms: `1 I` 0.284, `1 l` 0.327 and `1 |` 0.333 (target 0.40; T26), `O D` 0.168, `5 S` 0.200, `g q` 0.317, `' \`` 0.324, `( {` 0.284 and `} ]` 0.287 (0.35), `u υ` 0.130, `p ρ` 0.180, `θ 0` 0.234, `∨ v` 0.143, `⟨ (` 0.182 (and 0.180 in the proportional family, T27), `⊆ ⊊` 0.226, and in the proportional Regular `rn m` 0.231 (kerning leaves the pair alone, so this is the arm of `r`). `- −` and `− –` are near in every face; whether they should have a target at all is open.
- **G18 · What the distance measure and its snapshot do not see.** The measure centres two characters on their advances in the proportional family, so a change of a sign's sidebearings moves its distance to a neighbour without any change of shape (T27: `⟨ (` went from 0.235 to 0.180 when the bracket lost its cell); a pair of delimiters would be better compared as it stands against the same following letter. The snapshot holds the nine upright masters; the italics, which are the same programs sheared, are not measured.

- **G19 · Rachis Math has no script and no Fraktur alphabet.** `𝒜` to `𝓏` and `𝔄` to `𝔷`, their bold forms, and the letterlike `ℋ ℒ ℛ ℌ ℑ ℜ` they share with G5 are not drawn, so a calligraphic or Fraktur letter in a formula is set in another font (`proofs/formulas.typ` shows it). They are designs of their own, not the family's letters with other parameters, which is why T30 did not make them. The double-struck letters are the family's.
- **G20 · What Rachis Math's table does not state, and what does not stretch.** No `MathKern`, so a script is not moved into the corner of `A`, `V`, `f` or `P`. No flattened accents (`flac`) over capitals. The radical, the angle brackets `⟨ ⟩` and `⟪ ⟫`, `⟦ ⟧` and the slashes have sizes only (to 6500 and 6000 units) and no assembly. An assembled delimiter has the hooks of the text's size, so a parenthesis taller than 6000 units has smaller turns than the size below it. The tilde below (U+0330) has no wider size, and the harpoons over letters and the arrows under them (U+20D0, U+20D1, U+20EE, U+20EF) are not drawn. `⇝` has four sizes and cannot be assembled. `rachis check` compares masters and Rachis Math has one, so its derived glyphs are checked only for a program that fails (which fails the build), and none of them is in the distance snapshot.
- **G21 · Rachis Math has one weight and has been set by three typesetters.** A bold formula takes its letters from the bold alphabets and keeps the Regular's signs. The font has been read in Typst 0.15.1, LuaLaTeX (unicode-math 0.8r) and Chromium's MathML; not in XeLaTeX, ConTeXt, Firefox, Safari or Word's equation editor. In Chromium a brace or an arrow stretched over a long group is cut short at one end at some lengths, with Latin Modern Math, Lete Sans Math and XITS Math as with Rachis Math (seen on 2026-11-15 by pointing the `@font-face` of `proofs/mathml.html` at each). ttfautohint hints the static font; its tall sizes were compared hinted and unhinted from 12 to 28 px in FreeType only (G7).

### Ideas

- **I2 · Apertures as a parameter**, more open at the small optical size.
- **I3 · A cursive italic** as a stylistic set, for emphasis in prose (T08 keeps the sloped roman as the default).
- **I4 · Case-sensitive forms** (`case`), fractions from figures (`frac`), old-style figures.
- **I5 · A live builder in the specimen**: the kernel compiled to WebAssembly, drawing a glyph as its parameters are moved.
- **I6 · Proportional figures** (`pnum`) for the proportional family (T19).
- **I7 · Texture healing** in the fixed pitch (T06 rejects it as contextual; recorded because it is the known alternative).
- **I8 · Cyrillic** (G5).
- **I9 · Named numbers a trial can set from the command line** (`rachis measure --check --set comma.tail=0.85`), so that a sweep of a shape needs no rebuild. T28's sweeps were done with temporary variables read from the environment; the snapshot check already reports rises and falls in a second.
