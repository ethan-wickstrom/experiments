# Ledger

Every open item of the typeface (a gap, an idea, a deferral, a question, a request to the owner) and every closed one with its resolution. An entry's status changes in the same commit as the change that affects it; entries are closed, never deleted. G is a gap in what exists, I an idea not started, REQ a request to the owner.

## Open

### Gaps

- **G2 · Kerning and mark anchors do not vary across the design space.** They are computed at the Regular of the text size and applied at every weight and optical size, and in the static family of the small size (T17, T20). The compiler would store per-master values as deltas; `kern::values` already computes them per master (`rachis kern --own` shows them: To is −61 in the Bold, where the fonts have −77).
- **G3 · A few glyphs of some static fonts keep overlapping contours**, because the boolean union went wrong on them and the raster check caught it (T23): 46 glyph instances of 51,670 on 2026-11-15 (42 before the signs of the proportional family were widened), listed by `rachis build`. Moving the contours apart by a hair, and uniting them one at a time, were tried and cured none.
- **G4 · Glyphs their authors called weak**, to be redrawn: `⎈ ⎋ ⌘ ⌨` in the Bold; `⅒`; `ₘ ᵐ` at 13 px in the Bold; subscripts with descenders reach −272, below the line box; `℘ ℜ 𝕎 𝕨 ℑ ℅`; `ϑ Ϗ ν`; `⇔ ⇕ ⟺` in the Bold at 13 to 16 px, `↝ ↜ ↭ ⇴ ⇼ ↠` in the Bold; `⊰ ⊱ ≼ ≽ ⋞ ⋟ ⟕ ⟖ ⟗ ⩴ ⋎ ⊜ ∜`; `æ` in the fixed pitch, `ð`, `Ǻ ǻ`, the cedilla's hook; the control pictures, `₧ ₨ ✂ ❅ ₾`; the Powerline branch sign at 13 px, the spinner frames, the shades at 13 to 14 px. `ϵ` and `∈` are close at 13 px.
- **G5 · Characters of the coverage survey that are not drawn**: 204 of the 873 of tier 2 (`research/`; tier 1's 1405 are all drawn): Cyrillic (124); arrows `⤊ ⤋ ⟰ ⟱ ⇮ ⇯ ⇶ ↫ ↬ ↯ ➦ ⟴`; the script capitals `ℊ ℋ ℐ ℒ ℛ ℬ ℰ ℱ ℳ ℴ 𝒫`, the Fraktur `ℌ ℨ ℭ`, `ℼ ⅀ ℶ ℷ ℸ`, the Ångström sign; the three-letter control pictures (20), `₯ ☈`; `⎉ ⟅ ⟆`; U+034D; `ϐ` and the archaic Greek numerals; the Roman numerals `Ⅰ` to `Ⅵ`, `↊ ↋`; the enclosed letters.
- **G6 · Box drawing at small sizes.** Where a line's edge falls part-way across a pixel, the overshoot of two neighbouring cells covers it twice and the join shows as a darker bead (T22); the shades alias into a plaid at 13 to 17 px. Hinting the lines to the pixel grid would cure the first.
- **G7 · Hinting.** The static fonts are hinted by ttfautohint and have been looked at only through FreeType; nothing has been looked at on Windows. The variable fonts are unhinted.
- **G8 · fontbakery's findings on the final fonts** (`check-universal`, version 1.1.0, on the two upright variable fonts, 2026-11-15: 181 PASS, 13 WARN, 6 FAIL, no ERROR). Four FAILs come from checking two families in one run (family names and axes differ between Rachis and Rachis Mono, as they should). The fonts' own: `usWinAscent` 1000 and `usWinDescent` 250 are less than the 1100 and 350 that box-drawing lines and bracket pieces reach, because those overshoot the line box so that neighbouring lines join (T13, T22); where a renderer clips at the win metrics it clips only the overshoot. Warnings to look at: the start points of one contour of `⇳` and of `⦾` differ between the Light and the Bold; `uni0312.case` is not reachable; `numberOfHMetrics` is 1983 in the fixed pitch because zero-advance marks are not first in the glyph order; no `avar` (the axes are linear); the carons of `ď ľ ť Ľ` want a look by eye. The italics and the static fonts have not been run.
- **G14 · Tests of the compiler cover the proportional face only through `build.rs`** (axes, advances, GPOS features, the kerning's behaviour). No test reads a kerning value back from a compiled font, or compiles the italics or a static font of Rachis.
- **G15 · Kerning of punctuation against letters is crude**: a colon before o is closed by 30 units because only the rows of its two dots count.
- **G16 · The combining overline, low line and long solidus attach only to letters and figures** (the `rule` anchor of the modules lower, upper, digits, greek, latin, letterlike). After a sign or an arrow they are still set over the following character in shaped text.
- **G9 · The proportional family's details.** Parentheses, brackets and braces are the fixed pitch's shapes, wide for a text face; `ď ľ ť` are loose; the Greek letters' widths are stated as cells (`Fit::letter`), not as sidebearings and ink, and the tail of `ρ` hangs into the sidebearing; the three-per-em and other spaces are not kerned against. Signs that still have the figure's cell after T27 and were not looked at: the keys and interface signs (`⌘ ⌥ ⏎ ⌫` and the rest of `technical.rs` but its delimiters), the pictographs of `shapes.rs`, `℮ ℅ ⅋ ℧ ℞ 𝕎 ℵ ℘` among the letterlike signs, `‴ ⁗ ⁄ ‿ ⁀ ‽`, `#` and `¶`. The combining arrows and dots over letters (U+20D6, U+20D7, U+20E1, U+20DB, U+20DC) are the fixed pitch's size, about two thirds of what mathematics fonts draw. The multiple integrals `∬ ∭ ∯ ∰` have their ink between the margins and their strokes are still packed as in the cell. Signs are not kerned against letters or each other.
- **G10 · Proofing coverage.** The Small masters and the Text Light and Bold were looked at as lines of text, not glyph by glyph; many glyphs drawn on 2026-11-15 were proofed in the Regular and the Bold of the fixed pitch only (each module's author listed what was not proofed).
- **G11 · The proof tool draws a zero-advance mark in the cell after its base**, so bare combining marks cannot be proofed in place.
- **G13 · The specimen page still copies some numbers by hand** (`specimen/specimen.html`): the lowest and highest reference distance of each pair in its chart (from `measurements.md`; they change only if the references do), the glyph counts and the time of a build in its terminal block. - **G18 · What the distance measure and its snapshot do not see.** The measure centres two characters on their advances in the proportional family, so a change of a sign's sidebearings moves its distance to a neighbour without any change of shape (T27: `⟨ (` went from 0.235 to 0.180 when the bracket lost its cell); a pair of delimiters would be better compared as it stands against the same following letter. ### Ideas

- **I1 · An OpenType MATH table** (T25): constants, italic corrections, accent attachment, and delimiters, radicals and integrals of any size made by running their programs at other heights.
- **I2 · Apertures as a parameter**, more open at the small optical size.
- **I3 · A cursive italic** as a stylistic set, for emphasis in prose (T08 keeps the sloped roman as the default).
- **I4 · Case-sensitive forms** (`case`), fractions from figures (`frac`), old-style figures.
- **I5 · A live builder in the specimen**: the kernel compiled to WebAssembly, drawing a glyph as its parameters are moved.
- **I6 · Proportional figures** (`pnum`) for the proportional family (T19).
- **I7 · Texture healing** in the fixed pitch (T06 rejects it as contextual; recorded because it is the known alternative).
- **I8 · Cyrillic** (G5).
- **I9 · Named numbers a trial can set from the command line** (`rachis measure --check --set comma.tail=0.85`), so that a sweep of a shape needs no rebuild. T28's sweeps were done with temporary variables read from the environment; the snapshot check already reports rises and falls in a second.

## Closed

- **Review of pull request 58** by an independent agent that saw the diff and the repository (2026-11-15): twelve findings, all verified. Fixed in the same pull request: accented letters took their base's kerning, so `Tî` was closed by 77 units and the accent ran into the bar (each accented letter is now kerned by its own outline: `Tî` +28); the static family Rachis Small had no kerning (it has the face's); `Ĳ` kerned as `I` on its right (now as `J`); pairs whose inks share no row were closed (`:-` was −74, now 0); the proof tool and `rachis kern` showed each master's own kerning, which the fonts do not have (they show the fonts'; `--own` shows the master's); the counter of `⦾` and the wedges of `⟎ ⟏` closed and turned round in the Bold, which `rachis check` did not see (fixed, and the check now reports a contour whose size passes through zero); glyph programs that reach themselves overflowed the stack (they are reported; the second review and Codex showed two ways the first guard failed, below); the rule against branching on slant was stricter than the code (the rule now says what must not vary within a face); T19 and the specimen's note on optical size overstated; a test's name claimed more than it asserted. What stays open from it: G14, G15.
- **Second independent review, of the fixes, and Codex's review of pull request 58** (2026-11-15), all verified and fixed before the merge. From the second review: a real cycle inside a letter made `rachis check` hang, because every level above the limit ran its program again for its fit and its anchors (every program still waiting is now cut short once the limit is hit, and a test runs the cycle with a fit and anchors installed); the test for a contour turned round missed one that closes in one direction only (the check now asks whether the contour still has a shape half-way between two masters and whether its points stay near their places); a pair's kerning could differ from its own measure by 19 units through the classes' tolerance (the tolerance is 5 units now, 10 at most for a pair); spacing accents were kerned (left out); a median in T26 was 0.264, not 0.265. From Codex: U+0305, U+0332 and U+0338 had no anchors and were set over the following character (letters and figures now carry a `rule` anchor, and shaping puts the three over their base); a glyph that is its own component overflowed the stack in `flat` (the traversal counts its depth and the check resolves components); the distances of `rn m`, `cl d` and `vv w` were measured without the fonts' kerning (they are measured with it). Measuring `cl` with its kerning showed that closing it by 30 units was wrong, which led to the rule that two lowercase letters are never closed (T17).
- **The snapshot check took 96 seconds** because the fits of the superellipse were cached behind one lock that the threads drawing the masters queued for; the cache is per thread now and the check takes 1.3 seconds (`crates/kernel/src/geom.rs`). A first version of the cycle guard counted depth across threads and dropped glyphs at random under the same threads; it counts per thread.

- **Reviews of pull request 59** (2026-11-15) by an independent agent that saw the diff and the repository, and by Codex. All findings were verified and fixed in the pull request. From the agent: a sign built on another sign drew what it added about the middle of its own cell, while its component stood where its own room had put it, so the slashes of `∤ ∦` stood beside their bars and those of `⊬ ⊭ ⊮ ⊯` off-centre (the addition is now placed against the component's ink, and a test holds every such sign that the fixed pitch centres to the same in the proportional family; the test found `⋢ ⋣` as well, which the review's comparison had missed, because a glyph looked up from inside a fitted glyph was built with that glyph's cell: it is now built with the master's); `∢` had a room its `<` did not (it keeps the cell); a sign's growth was derived from the cell's width, which programs that draw a letter in a narrower cell also change (it is a value only the registry sets now, `Params::grown`); two characters had rooms and no glyphs (removed, and a test asks that every room has a glyph); the trials listed in T28 could not be rerun (they are labelled as one-off results); the snapshot commands ignored other options, would have passed a distance that is not a number, and held only the Regular masters (they refuse other options, fail on it, and hold every upright master).
