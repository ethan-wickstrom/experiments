Read with it

This page is set in the family. The sample below can be edited, and the controls change how it is set. Rachis is for paragraphs, formulas, captions and footnotes; Rachis Mono is for code and terminals. Both come from the same glyph programs, so a sentence, a formula and a line of code share one x-height and one set of strokes.

Told apart, and measured

Characters that technical text confuses differ in outline, because at reading size an inner mark is a weak cue. Each pair is rendered at 14 px, blurred as the eye blurs it, and the difference of the two images is measured (0: identical; 1.41: nothing in common). Mathematics

336 operators and relations, 140 arrows, Greek, double-struck letters, delimiters, and superscripts and subscripts that are drawn at their own size with strokes of their own, not scaled down. Signs sit on one axis and are built from the same bar, chevron, stem and ring as the ASCII operators, so -> and → have the same height and weight. In Rachis Mono every sign has the cell. In Rachis the signs of arithmetic are as wide as a figure and every other sign has the room its shape wants: an arrow is 1.9 times as long as a plus is wide, a relation between sets 1.3 times.

Formulas

Rachis Math is the proportional Regular with an OpenType MATH table: the font a formula typesetter reads its constants, its sizes and its pieces from. Your browser is setting the formulas below from MathML with it. Nothing in it was drawn again. A variable is the italic’s letter, a tall parenthesis is the parenthesis’s program run with taller brackets, a display integral is the integral’s program run larger with hooks that grow more slowly than its height, and an arrow or a brace of any length is assembled from pieces cut out of the sign’s own outline. A script has a form of its own, the design for 8 point at a heavier weight, so an exponent of an exponent keeps strokes that can be read.

Rachis Math has 3,458 glyphs, fourteen sizes of each delimiter up to 6 em tall, and 47 arrows, bars and braces that are assembled to any length. It was set in Typst, in LuaLaTeX and from MathML until each set it right; they read a font differently, and the repository’s proofs/ has the sheets. A script capital is the capital written by hand with the capitals’ own pen: it leans 16 degrees where the italic leans 10 and has a hook wherever a stroke begins or ends, each large enough to be seen at 13 pixels, and a test holds every one of them apart from its italic, its upright and every other script capital. The font has one weight, and no Fraktur alphabet yet.

Code and terminals

Rachis Mono has no ligatures and nothing contextual: what is on the screen is what is in the file. Box-drawing lines keep one thickness in every weight and join across cells and lines; blocks divide the cell exactly. Powerline separators, the branch-drawing set, sextants and Braille are included.

9 px, design for 16

A metric space X is compact if every open cover of X has a finite subcover. In ℝᵏ this holds exactly for the closed and bounded sets (Heine–Borel); in general a set is compact if and only if it is complete and totally bounded. Continuous images of compact sets are compact, so a continuous f: X → ℝ on a compact X attains its supremum: sup f = f(x₀) for some x₀ ∈ X, with |f(x) − f(y)| < ε whenever d(x, y) < δ(ε).

How a letter is made

A glyph is a function. It places knots, each a point of the skeleton with the breadth of the pen there; the centre line through them is Hobby’s spline or a superellipse of exponent 5/2; the outline is that line offset to both sides. Weight, optical size, slant and pitch are parameters, so one function draws its letter in eighteen masters, and the masters have the same structure by construction.

                   p.xh + p.over, 0.0,

Kerning is computed too: the white between two letters is measured on every row where they stand side by side, and pairs with more of it than two stems have are closed by part of the excess. T and o: −77 units. A and V: −80. r and n: 0, so that the pair is not read as m.
