# One afternoon

The question an afternoon answers: does this problem deserve a week? Not whether the fix works. Not whether it lasts.

## Before asking

1. **The problem, in the words of the person who has it.** Who has it, what they do about it now, and what that costs them in a month.
2. **The people.** Only people who have already said they have this problem. Write down each one's tie to the asker: stranger, acquaintance, friend, customer. No list of such people means no afternoon. The work is then to find where they say it, not to pitch.
3. **The stake.** What a yes costs them, chosen before asking. In order of strength:
   - money: a deposit, returned in full if the fix is not in their hands by a stated date;
   - a change to their work: they send real data, or switch one real task over;
   - time: a booked working session with their real material.
   A reply, a signup, a "sounds great" and a survey answer are not stakes.
4. **The rule.** How many yeses out of how many asks earn the week. Fixed now.
5. **The guess.** `python3 verdict.py bet ...` with the probability that the rule is met.

## Asking

- One message each, at most one reminder. At most 20 people.
- The ask states the problem, the stake, the delivery date and what they get back if the date is missed.
- Nobody is paid or rewarded for answering. A paid yes is not a yes.
- No simulated buyers, and no model's estimate of what people would pay, at any step.

## Counting

- A yes counts when the stake is in hand: the deposit is paid, the data has arrived, the session is in the calendar.
- A yes from a friend is recorded and not counted. A person who would say yes to the asker whatever was asked says nothing about the problem.
- Silence is a no.
- The rule decides. A near miss is a no.

## After

- Record the verdict on the bet, with the evidence.
- Yes: the asker is now bound too. The delivery date and the refund hold.
- No: write down what the people did instead. That is the cheapest thing learned all week.

## The week and the six weeks

- **A week:** the fix is in real daily use by the people who said yes. Changes that can be undone ship without review. A change that sends someone a message or touches their money waits for the person whose name is on it.
- **Six weeks:** one outcome decides, named on day one, that neither the asker nor an agent can raise by trying: a second payment, or use on day 42 that nobody prompted. The early numbers guide the work. This one judges it.
